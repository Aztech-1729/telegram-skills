# Primary accessibility sources

Reviewed **2026-10-04**. W3C Understanding pages explain criteria; the normative
standard is [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). No legal compliance or
universal-client conclusion follows from this documentation review.

- [Contrast minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) and [use of color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html).
- [Target size minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) and [reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html).
- [Focus not obscured minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html) and [focus appearance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html): AA versus AAA distinction.
- [Error identification](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/error-identification.html) and [status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html).
- [W3C modal dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/): keyboard/focus behavior for a custom web dialog.
- [Telegram Mini Apps](https://core.telegram.org/bots/webapps): host controls and theme/version boundaries.

The contrast formula in the helper follows WCAG relative luminance for sRGB;
design choices such as 44px controls and copy examples are recommendations.
